OUR FARM

Rooted in Karoi.

Blockley Farm Enterprises is based in Karoi, Zimbabwe. We raise layers, poultry and livestock and grow vegetables and field crops. Enquire about eggs, birds, cattle, goats, pigs, maize and soya; availability changes with the season and production cycle.

We welcome supply enquiries for both smaller orders and bulk needs. Tell us what you need, roughly how much, where it is going and when you need it; our team will confirm what we can supply.

ORDERING & DELIVERY

How to order.

Choose the contact for your product above and tell us the item, quantity, preferred date and destination. We will confirm current stock, price, lead time and whether collection or delivery can be arranged for that order.

For bulk orders or farm transport, request a quotation before making plans. Minimum quantities and payment terms are confirmed directly with the relevant team. We currently deliver to Karoi town; deliveries to Chinhoyi, Harare and Kariba may be arranged when order volumes make a route practical.
